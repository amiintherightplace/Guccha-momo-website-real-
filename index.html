Our story

A small brick doorway, a very good dumpling.

Tucked into Pyapal, steps from the square, Guccha Momo is a cafe built around one thing: momos you can eat in a single bite, finished with a signature achar that regulars come back for.

Order the fokso fry beside your plate, then climb to the top floor. Visitors single it out for the views of the square.

  • Bite-sized momos, made to order
  • House achar, our signature
  • Everyday prices, most dishes under Rs 500
